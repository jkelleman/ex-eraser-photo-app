# Settings

Settings let users shape the pace and tone of the experience. Edit labels and on-device processing are always on and can't be turned off, since they protect against misuse and keep the privacy promise.

| Setting | Options | Default | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| People | List of people being faded · Add someone | — | Supports more than one person, such as an ex-friend |
| Tone | Gentle · Brief | Gentle | Brief uses fewer words for people who want to move fast |
| Suggestions | On · Off | On | Off means the user picks every mode themselves |
| Batch size | 10 · 20 · 50 | 20 | |
| Batch order | Easiest first · Hardest first · By date | Easiest first | |
| Break reminders | Every 15 min · Every 30 min · Off | Every 15 min | |
| Replace style | Silhouette · Pet · Object | Silhouette | Pet uses a photo of the user's own pet that they choose |
| Shared children | Fade only · Let me change them one at a time | Fade only | Bulk changes always skip these photos |
| Face data | Erase now | — | Face data is already erased after each session. This button is there for reassurance |
| Start over | Restore everything | — | Undoes every change for every person |

## Copy

*Changing the shared-children setting:* "Photos with your children and [name] can now be changed one at a time. Bulk changes will still skip them."

**Buttons:** Turn on · Cancel

*Edit labels row:* "Edited photos are always labeled in their details. This keeps it clear which photos were changed."

*Erase face data:* "Face data erased. Nothing about [name]'s face is stored on this phone."

*Stop fading someone:* "Stop hiding [name]? Their photos will show up in Memories again. Edited photos stay edited until you restore them in the vault."

**Buttons:** Stop hiding · Cancel

*Start over:* "Restore every photo and clear all your choices? Your originals will come back, and the app will be like new."

**Buttons:** Restore everything · Cancel
