# Onboarding

App copy is in quotes. Buttons are listed after each step. Notes on logic are in italics.

## Script

### 1. Welcome

"Welcome to [App]. This is a space to keep your good memories while seeing less of someone you're moving on from."

"You're in control of every change, and your original photos are always kept safe."

**Buttons:** Get started · How does it work?

### 2. Photo access

"To find photos of this person, [App] needs access to your photo library."

"Face matching happens only on this phone. Face data is never uploaded, never shared, and is erased when you finish each session."

**Buttons:** Allow access · Not now

*If "Not now":* "That's fine. You can come back whenever you're ready."

### 3. Choose the person

"Who would you like to see less of?"

*Show face groups from the Photos People album.*

**Buttons:** [face thumbnails] · I don't see them

*After they pick:* "What would you like me to call them? You can skip this."

**Buttons:** [text field] · Skip

### 4. Check in

"Thank you. Before we start, how are you feeling about this today?"

**Buttons:** Ready to sort through photos · I just need a break from seeing them · I'm not sure yet

*If "a break" or "not sure":* "A gentle place to start is Fade. Their photos stay exactly as they are, and they'll stop showing up in Memories, widgets and slideshows. You can do more later, or undo it anytime."

**Buttons:** Fade their photos · Show me all the options

### 5. Shared children

"One more question. Do you and [name] share children?"

**Buttons:** Yes · No · Skip

*If "Yes":* "Thank you for telling me. Photos with your children and [name] will only be faded. If you ever want to change one of those photos, you can choose that one photo at a time. They're never included in bulk changes."

**Buttons:** That makes sense

### 6. The options

"Here's what [App] can do. You can mix these, photo by photo."

- **Fade.** Their photos stop appearing in Memories, widgets and slideshows. Nothing is edited.
- **Crop.** Trim them out of the frame.
- **Erase.** Remove them and fill in the background.
- **Replace.** Swap them for a pet, an object or a soft silhouette.
- **Delete.** Remove a photo. You'll always get a final review, and it stays recoverable for 30 days.

"Every edit is labeled in the photo's details, and your original is kept."

**Buttons:** Start reviewing · Fade everything for now

### 7. First batch

"I found 312 photos with [name]. Let's look at the first 20."

"I've suggested an option for each one. Change anything that doesn't feel right."

*Footer on every review screen:* Take a break

*After the batch:* "That's 20 done. How would you like to continue?"

**Buttons:** Next 20 · Done for today

### 8. Closing

"You did something hard today. Your changes are saved, and your originals are safe."

"Come back whenever you like. Nothing is final for 30 days."

**Buttons:** Close

## How does it work?

A four-card carousel opened from the welcome screen. Each card has one idea and one or two sentences. The user can swipe through or tap Get started at any point.

### Card 1: Find

"You choose the person. [App] finds their photos using face matching on this phone."

### Card 2: Choose

"For each photo, you decide how much to change. Fade it, crop it, erase them, replace them, or delete it. I'll suggest an option to start."

### Card 3: Keep

"Your originals are kept in a private vault, so you can restore any photo whenever you want. Every edited photo is labeled in its details."

### Card 4: Private

"Everything happens on this phone. Nothing is uploaded, and face data is erased after each session."

**Buttons:** Next · Get started (on the last card: Get started only)
