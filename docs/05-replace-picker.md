# Replace picker

Replace puts something gentle where the person was. The user picks from three types: a silhouette (the default), their own pet, or an everyday object. The app never adds people to photos, which keeps memories honest and protects everyone's privacy.

## Layout

- A bottom sheet with three choices across the top: Silhouette · Pet · Object.
- A live preview of the photo above the sheet updates with each choice.
- The user can always switch to Erase or Fade from the sheet.
- Replaced photos get the same "Edited with generative AI" label as erased ones.

## Object list

Objects stay calm and kind, in keeping with the healing tone: a bouquet of flowers, a potted plant, a balloon, a coffee cup, a teddy bear, a stack of books. Joke objects, like a red flag, are held back for a possible playful tone later.
